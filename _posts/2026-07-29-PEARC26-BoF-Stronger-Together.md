abstract: >
    There are collections of HPC online training materials and events at computing centers, national labs, universities, corporations, and more. This plethora of portals makes sense; any given site will focus on specific resources, disciplines, and site-specific procedures. Our goal for this BoF is that collection curators could learn from each other; we could improve our collections as well as work together to improve the results and experience for the learner. And finally, by learning more about each other, we may find ways to avoid redundancy and work together. We expect this BoF to attract collection owners, but insights and opinions from those who use collections would be very helpful as well.
